# Voice Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Peer-to-peer voice inside a room: opt-in voice, local mute and deafen of yourself and others, per-person voice volume, separate voice devices, and the UI described in the spec.

**Architecture:** Voice reuses the application-audio machinery end to end: `AudioPublisher` encodes the microphone, the media server grants a new `SubscribeVoice` subscription on the existing per-member connection, and each listener runs an `AudioViewer` into a voice-only `Mixer` with its own output stream. A new `voice` module in `brp-room` owns the rules, the sending side (gating each subscriber against presence and local deafen), and the listening side (one listener per person in voice). The app adds commands, settings, and the dock, rail, map, tile and settings UI.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 1.95, iroh/QUIC, postcard, FFmpeg libopus, cpal 0.18, egui 0.36.

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-voice-design.md](../specs/2026-11-05-voice-design.md)

## Global Constraints

- Opus in mono, VoIP mode, 20 ms frames, `VOICE_BITRATE_KBPS = 32`. Everything above the codec stays 48 kHz stereo (`AudioParams::STANDARD`, `AudioFrame`, jitter buffer, mixer).
- Wire compatibility: new `Presence` fields go after `quality_presets` with `absent_when_truncated`; new enum variants go after every existing variant; `MEDIA_ALPN` does not change. A peer whose presence has `voice: None` is never sent a voice request or a voice frame.
- Muting someone keeps their subscription and drops their packets before decoding. Deafening someone sends them encoded silence at the normal packet rate. Nothing in brp shows either to the other person, and no UI shows a count of who hears you.
- The microphone is open exactly while you are in voice and unmuted, independent of subscribers.
- Voice has its own `Mixer` and output stream. The master mute, renamed **Mute streams**, never reaches voice.
- Joining voice starts unmuted and undeafened, by click or automatically.
- Settings: `auto_join_voice` off by default; voice output is the audio output unless `separate_voice_output` is set; voice device changes apply on the next join.
- Exact copy: `Mute streams`, `Voice`, `on air`, `muted`, `deafened`, `no microphone`, `speaking`, `Hear {name}`, `{name} stops hearing you`, `{name} joined voice`, `{name} left voice`, `Mute microphone` / `Unmute microphone`, `Deafen` / `Undeafen`, `Join voice automatically`, `Your microphone goes live in every room you enter`, `Microphone`, `Voice output`, `Same as audio output`, `No microphone. Check your input device or choose another in Settings, then turn Voice off and on. ({error})`.
- Colour: alarm only for failure (`no microphone`, the notice); `signal` only for what is flowing (on air, speech bars, voice packets); muted and deafened states are never red.
- Commits: Conventional Commits with a scope, as in `git log` (`feat(net): …`). No attribution trailers. Do not push: the next non-`fix:` push to `main` releases.
- Every new constant goes in `crates/proto/src/constants.rs` (wire and timing values) or beside its only user (UI values), with a one-line reason for its value.

## Review Focus

1. **A microphone with no 48 kHz input, or a saved microphone that is unplugged.** Joining must still put you in voice, listening, shown muted with `no microphone`, never panic. Covered by Task 3's `an_input_without_48_khz_is_refused_by_name` and Task 8's `a_missing_microphone_leaves_you_in_voice_listening_and_muted`.
2. **Clicking Voice twice quickly.** A second join while joined is a no-op: one output stream, one publisher. Covered by Task 8's `joining_twice_opens_one_output`.
3. **Someone leaves the room mid-conversation.** Their listener stops and their forwarder ends, with nothing left in either mixer. Covered by Task 9's `a_member_who_leaves_the_room_leaves_no_listener_behind`.
4. **An older peer in the room.** It shows as not in voice and is never asked for voice. Covered by Task 1's presence compatibility test and Task 5's `nobody_out_of_voice_is_listened_to`.
5. **Unmuting while the microphone is broken.** You stay muted and the error stays, rather than the mute toggle flickering. Covered by Task 8's `unmuting_with_a_broken_microphone_stays_muted`.
